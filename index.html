A number settled by telegram

Planners first wanted the Chicago to Los Angeles road to be Route 60. Kentucky objected. On April 30, 1926, Oklahoma's Cyrus Avery and Missouri's B. H. Piepmeier sent a telegram from Springfield, Missouri, proposing 66 instead.
